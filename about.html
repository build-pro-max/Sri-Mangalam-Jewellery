Our story

A shop, not a showroom

There is no lift, no atrium and no queue token at Sri Mangalam. There is a counter, a chair, and someone who will open as many trays as you want to see before you decide anything.

30, Cross Cut Road · Gandhipuram · Coimbatore

How we came to keep what we keep

Our trays are shaped by the people who walk in. A family planning a wedding wants weight and temple work that photographs well. A working daughter wants a chain that survives a scooter ride and a kitchen. A grandmother buying for a first birthday wants bangles small enough to slip on, thick enough to keep.

So we buy for those three people, not for a catalogue. If something is not in the case, we can usually get it made — and we will tell you honestly how long that takes.
