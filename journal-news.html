New in the trays this season

Lighter harams, cut-work bangles and a fuller silver counter for the festival months.

What sits in our cases changes through the year, mostly because of what people ask for. Here is what has come in for this season, and what is worth planning ahead for.

Lighter harams for reception wear

The clearest shift this year: brides want a second, lighter neck piece rather than a second full set. We have brought in long malais at noticeably lower weights that still read well in photographs — openwork centres, fewer solid links, and clasps set to hang clear of a draped saree.

Cut-work bangles

Pierced cut-work bands are back on the counter in several patterns. They give the presence of a broad bangle at a fraction of the weight, which makes them a sensible everyday pair for someone who does not want to wear something heavy to work.

A fuller silver counter

For the festival and housewarming months we have widened the silver selection: kuthuvilakku in two heights, engraved pooja plates, kumkum sets, and boxed baby gift sets kept ready to hand over. Crystal and glass gift boxes sit alongside them for families who prefer something for the shelf.

If you know your function date, tell us now. Made-to-order slots fill up long before the season does.

Made-to-order slots

Karigar time is the one thing we cannot stock. Custom temple work, stone setting and heavy bridal pieces need weeks, and those weeks disappear first in wedding season. Send the design on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will hold a slot for it.

Savings scheme

If a wedding or a function is a year away, the monthly scheme is worth asking about at the counter. Put aside a fixed amount each month and buy at the end without one large payment landing in the same month as the hall and the catering.

Have a question about your own piece?

Send a photo on WhatsApp, or bring it to the counter on Cross Cut Road. We will tell you what it is and what it needs.
